## Rule and media findings

- The initial Rules allowed user-selected privileged roles and self-written memberships; profile creation and role/membership mutation are now denied to clients.
- Initial listing authorization was owner-UID-only. Listing, staff, vendor session, stock movement, tour and audit mutations now use Functions or are denied at the client Rules boundary.
- Approved stores/promotions and published catalog data are publicly readable; store applications, unapproved promotions and draft tours are readable only by their owner/admin. Tour writes now use a callable; publication remains blocked pending a sanitized derivative. Vendor coordinates are public only while a timestamped session is unexpired and not ended.
- Storage originals remain private, upload paths are UID-scoped and size/MIME limited, and clients cannot write processed/public derivatives. Panorama finalize validation is present, but privacy sanitization is not.
- Firestore rules are not default-deny for modeled collections; there is no Rules Emulator test suite in the repository.

## Immediate change made

Self-registration now always results in a customer-only profile through the Auth trigger. Firestore Rules deny client profile creation and limit self-updates to the name field, while role, memberships and grants remain server-managed.
